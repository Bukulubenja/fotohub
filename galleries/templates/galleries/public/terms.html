Buying photos: terms and refunds

How buying photos from a FotoHub gallery works, and what to do if something goes wrong.

What you buy

  • Each photo is the photographer's full-quality original file: {{ price_ugx }} by mobile money or {{ price_usd }} by card.
  • Previews in the gallery are smaller and watermarked. The original is only available after payment.

Paying

  • Pay once for every photo in your cart, by MTN or Airtel mobile money or by card. Payments are handled by Flutterwave; FotoHub never sees your card details.
  • Your photos unlock only after the payment provider confirms the payment to our server. Leaving the payment page early doesn't lose your order: open your order link to check its status.

Getting your photos

  • After payment, your order page lets you download each photo or all of them as a ZIP. We email you its link. The link is your key, so keep it private.
  • Your order link keeps working even if the photographer later takes the gallery down.
  • A prepared ZIP is kept for a day. After that, prepare a new one from your order page.

Problems and refunds

  • If you were charged but can't download your photos, or you were charged twice, contact {% if support_email %}FotoHub support{% else %}the photographer who shared the gallery{% endif %} with your order link. We'll check the payment with the provider and fix the order or arrange a refund.
  • For any other refund request, contact the photographer who shared the gallery.

Privacy and takedown

  • Galleries are private: only people with the link (and the password, if one is set) can open them. Please don't repost or share images of other people without their permission.
  • Need an image reviewed or removed? Contact the photographer who shared the gallery{% if support_email %} or FotoHub support{% endif %}.
